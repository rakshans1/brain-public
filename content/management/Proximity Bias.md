What is proximity bias
[Proximity bias](https://www.forbes.com/sites/forbestechcouncil/2022/04/04/how-to-mitigate-proximity-bias-and-create-a-more-inclusive-workplace-culture/) is a phenomenon in which employees who are physically closer to executive leaders enjoy outsized influence and advancement opportunities relative to those who are hybrid or fully remote.
