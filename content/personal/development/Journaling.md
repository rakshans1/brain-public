### Reflective journaling
Involves examining your thoughts, feelings, and experiences to gain deeper self-awareness. You might write about challenges you're facing, decisions you need to make, or insights you've gained.
Examples
- [[Wheel Of Life]]
### Gratitude journaling 
It focuses on recording things you're thankful for, which can help foster a more positive mindset and improve emotional well-being. This could be as simple as noting three good things that happened each day.

### Goal-oriented journaling 
Helps track progress toward personal or professional objectives. You might write about your aspirations, create action plans, or document milestones.
